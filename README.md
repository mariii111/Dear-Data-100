# Dear-Data-100

My project focuses on the diversity in City St Georges University based on culture and courses. The aim of my project is to observe how diverse City St George's is based on the different traditions, languages, and courses. 

To collect data, data will be collected every Tuesday and gather 10 data points each of the week to collect 100 data points. I will be asking individuals on their identity instead of assuming and answering the questionnaire will be voluntary.

My Data includes:
• Ethnicity: Asking what ethnicity they are
• Language: Asking if they speak their mother tongue
• Course: Asking what course they do & what year they are in
• Gender: Asking what gender they are

Data Visualisation
The data collected will be visually represented on a map to show the cultural diversity. Some elements may include different colours for each course, different shapes for gender and thickness of the outline represents the year of study they are in. These shapes will be put on the map depending on what country they are from. This visualisation will emphasise on storytelling and personal observation rather than only numerical data.
